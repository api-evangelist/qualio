Qualio is an AI-powered quality management system (QMS) and compliance platform built for life sciences companies — medical devices, pharmaceuticals, biotech, software as a medical device (SaMD), cosmetics, cannabis and contract research organizations. It unifies document control, quality events (CAPAs, deviations, non-conformances), training records, design controls, risk and change management, supplier management, audits and compliance intelligence in one audit-ready system. Qualio exposes a RESTful Developer API (OpenAPI 3.1.0, base URL https://api.qualio.com) providing secure, API-key-authenticated programmatic access to documents, events, training, users, suppliers, design controls and compliance data, letting teams automate quality processes and integrate the QMS with the rest of their toolchain.

**Areas:** Company · Quality Management · Life Sciences · Compliance · Document Control · Regulatory · Medical Devices

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Qualio: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
